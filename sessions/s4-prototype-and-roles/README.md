# Session 4 - From document to clickable prototype, and roles

**Theme of the live session:** a requirements document is hard to judge until you can click it. In this session you turn a BRD into a working prototype, then meet the idea of roles: a colleague with one job and limited access, who reads what you point to and returns a short report. A skill, by comparison, is a recipe: it tells you how to do something. In 4B you build the colleague as a read-only skill. A subagent is the same idea running as a separate helper in its own room, and in 4C you start three of them at once.

| Mission | What | Time | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4A | BRD to clickable prototype | 30 min | Core |
| 4B | Reviewer that can only read (a read-only skill) | 30-45 min | Stretch |
| 4C | Three checks in parallel, then merge | 30-45 min | Stretch |

Do 4A first. 4B and 4C can be done in any order.
