# Personas brief (background)

Background color on who might buy or use Signal. Not validated yet, that is partly your job. Use this in Missions 03, 05, 06, and 07.

---

## 1. "Derek" — Head of Product at a mid-size B2B SaaS (40-150 people)
- Time-poor, reactive, drowning in inputs from support, sales, and the CEO.
- Has a hunch about what matters but cannot prove it with data.
- Already pays for Productboard, barely uses it.
- Wants: a fresh, trustworthy "what to build next and why" without two weeks of manual tagging.
- Buys out of a product-tools budget. Can spend without a big procurement process at small contract sizes.
- Dealbreakers: wrong/hallucinated insights, no Zendesk + Slack integration, slow setup.
- **Likely our primary buyer.**

## 2. "Priya" — Support / CX Lead
- Sees pain first, wants early warning when an issue is blowing up.
- Influencer, not usually the budget owner. Would champion Signal internally.
- Wants: trend alerts, "this complaint is spiking."

## 3. "The Founder" — CEO of a 10-50 person startup
- Does not want a dashboard, wants a decision: "just tell me the 3 things."
- Forwards 11pm emails. Source of the reactive chaos.
- Wants: the one-slide summary, confidence the roadmap is grounded in real signal.
- May be the buyer at very small companies where there is no dedicated PM.

## 4. "Sam" — Solo PM at a Series A startup
- Wears every hat, zero time, no research function.
- The manual-tagging spreadsheet is a fantasy they will never have time for.
- Wants: connect a source, get value the same day, self-serve, cheap.
- Most underserved by existing (enterprise-priced, heavy) tools.

---

## What we do NOT know yet
- Which of these feels the pain most acutely and would pay first.
- Whether the buyer (Derek/Founder) and the heaviest user (Priya/Sam) are the same person.
- What they would actually pay, and whether it is a budgeted line item or a "nice to have."
- How big each of these segments is. (Mission 03 and 04 help here.)
