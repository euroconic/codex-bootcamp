# Competitor notes (rough, internal)

Quick notes gathered by the team. Not polished. Use these as a starting point for Mission 03. If you have internet access you can research further; if not, these are enough to work with.

---

## Productboard
- Category leader for product management / roadmapping.
- Strong at organizing feedback you have already tagged, and presenting roadmaps to stakeholders.
- Weak at the actual analysis: it does not read raw tickets and surface themes for you. A human still has to tag.
- Pricing: roughly $25-100 per maker seat / month depending on tier.
- Our read: customers buy it and then "barely use it" (see the Tilo call). Becomes a roadmap slideshow.

## Dovetail
- Research repository. Strong at qualitative analysis, tagging interview transcripts, building an insights library.
- Loved by dedicated UX researchers. Heavier tool, assumes you have a research practice.
- Pricing: per-seat, gets expensive for whole-team access.
- Our read: great for research teams, overkill and too manual for a time-poor head of product who just wants "the 3 things."

## Enterpret
- Newer, AI-first. Aggregates feedback from many sources and auto-categorizes it. Closest to our idea.
- Aimed up-market (larger companies), enterprise sales motion, pricing on request (not self-serve).
- Our read: validates the category and the AI approach. Gap: smaller/mid-market teams who want self-serve and same-day value, not a 6-week enterprise onboarding.

## Spreadsheets + manual tagging (the real competitor)
- What most teams actually do, or rather do once and abandon.
- Free, flexible, and produces genuinely useful insight.
- Fails on repeatability: it is a person-week of effort and the snapshot is stale the moment it is done.
- Our read: this is the status quo we are really fighting. The job is not "replace Productboard," it is "make the manual analysis automatic and repeatable."

## Generic AI chat (ChatGPT / Claude, used ad hoc)
- Some PMs paste batches of tickets into a chatbot and ask for themes.
- Works in a pinch but does not connect to sources, does not persist, no trust/traceability back to source tickets.
- Our read: proves people want AI to do this. Gap: it is not a workflow, it is a one-off hack.
