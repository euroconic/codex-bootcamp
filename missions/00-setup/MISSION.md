# Mission 00 — Setup & First Contact

> Mentor: present this conversationally. This is the learner's first ever contact with Codex. Go slow. The goal is for them to feel "oh, I just talk to it and it does things." Celebrate at the end, it is a real milestone.

## Goal
Get comfortable doing the four most basic things: looking around, reading a file, creating a file, and editing a file. By talking, not coding.

## Why it matters
Everything in this bootcamp (and your real job) is just files and conversations. Once you trust that you can ask the assistant to read, write, and change files for you, the rest is easy. This is the "hello world" of working with an AI teammate.

## What to do
Try each of these, one at a time. Type them in plain English, the mentor will do the action and explain what happened.

1. **Look around:** ask "what files are in this folder?" or "give me a tour of this project."
2. **Read something:** ask "read THE-CASE.md and explain it to me in 3 sentences."
3. **Create a file:** ask "create a file called my-notes.md in the workspace folder with my name, my role, and one thing I want to get out of this course."
4. **Edit a file:** ask "add a line to my-notes.md with today's date (any format is fine) and the words 'Day 1 done.'"

That is it. You just did the four core moves.

## Definition of Done
- A file exists at `workspace/my-notes.md`.
- It contains the learner's name/role/goal AND the edited "Day 1" line (proving they did both a create and an edit).

When that file is there, tell them they passed and to type `next`.

## Hints (mentor: reveal one at a time, only if asked)
1. Remind them they can just type a sentence, no special syntax needed.
2. If they are nervous, offer to do step 1 (the tour) together so they see it work once.
3. If they cannot find the workspace folder, explain it is the `workspace/` folder inside this project, and that is where all their work will live.

## What you just learned
You can ask an AI assistant to look around, read, create, and edit files for you in plain English. No menus, no code. That is 80% of what you will do all course.

There is no dumb question here. Ask your mentor anything, including "what does that mean" or "why did you do it that way." The shared Codex basics and interface-specific controls are in `START-HERE.md` whenever you want them.
